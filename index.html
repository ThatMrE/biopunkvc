In all its forms

What “earliest-stage capital” actually looks like

Biotech's first dollars rarely come from one place. The map covers the whole spectrum — dilutive and not.

// 01

Non-dilutive

Grants, fellowships, and prizes — from NIH SBIR and ARPA-H to philanthropic fast-grants. Money that funds the science without taking equity.

// 02

Company creation

Venture studios and creation firms that start the company with you — capitalizing a newco from an idea or an academic breakthrough.

// 03

First checks

Accelerators, pre-seed and seed funds, angels and syndicates writing the first equity or SAFE into a founding team.

// 04

Platform & space

Incubators and shared labs that provide the bench, reagents, and network that early biotech needs before it can scale.

What this is

A public map, not a pitch.

Biotech founders are hidden in labs — and so is the money that could start them. The earliest capital is scattered across hundreds of grants, accelerators, studios, fellowships, and first-check funds, each with its own stage, geography, and terms.

Biopunk exists to make that map legible and keep it current. It's an open, community-maintained directory of the earliest-stage financing for biotech, in every form — dilutive and non-dilutive, public and private, global.

Found a gap? Add a source or contribute on GitHub. The data is open.

Looking to raise from us?
